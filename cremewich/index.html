Our story

The cookie matters as much as the cream

Most ice cream sandwiches get one half right. The ice cream is good but the cookie is rock hard, or the cookie is lovely and goes soggy in a minute. Cremewich started with a simple goal: make both halves worth eating on their own, then put them together.

We bake our cookies soft enough to bite straight from the freezer and churn our ice cream in small batches so it stays dense and creamy. The result is the bite we kept chasing.

  • Baked fresh

    Cookies made in-house, soft enough to bite from frozen.

  • Small-batch churned

    Dense, creamy ice cream made a few batches at a time.

  • Real ingredients

    Real chocolate, real nuts, real fruit. Nothing you can't pronounce.
