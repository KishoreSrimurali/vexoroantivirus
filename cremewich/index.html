Our story

For the love of two favourites

Cremewich started with a simple belief: an ice cream sandwich should be more than a soggy wafer. So we bake our cookies thick, churn our ice cream dense, and press them together while both are at their best.

The result is the bite we kept chasing. We think it's perfect, but you should be the judge.

  • Baked daily

    Cookies come out of the oven every morning.

  • Slow-churned

    Dense, creamy ice cream made in small batches.

  • Hand-dipped

    Real chocolate shells, poured and set by hand.

  • Party ready

    Boxes and trays for every celebration.
